Frosted compact transport that rides above the tab bar (Apple Podcasts pattern). Tapping the bar opens Now Playing; the play button toggles playback.

```jsx
<MiniPlayer title="README.md" currentTime="1:23" duration="12:48" playing={isPlaying} onTogglePlay={toggle} onOpen={openNowPlaying} />
```

Pin it to the bottom safe area directly above a `TabBar`.
