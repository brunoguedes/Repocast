## Subhero

There's a lot of time in a week when your eyes are busy and your ears are free — the drive in, a long run, the gym. Repocast turns the repositories you work on into tracks for exactly that time: READMEs and design docs read aloud, source files explained in plain English, all generated on your iPhone.

## What it does

### Reads any repository you point it at

Paste a GitHub link or type `owner/repo` to add any public repository — no account needed. Connect a read-only GitHub token and you can browse your own repositories too, private ones included. Open folders, tick the files you want, tap Generate. Tracks land in your library, grouped by repository.

### Explains code instead of reciting it

Nobody wants to hear curly braces read aloud. For source files, Apple Intelligence writes a short spoken briefing — what the file does, its main types and functions, anything notable — and Repocast reads that instead. Long files are summarised in parts and joined into one narration. Markdown and plain text are read word for word. It all happens on your iPhone, with Apple's on-device model.

### Turns any text into audio

Paste anything — meeting notes, a spec, an article — and have it read verbatim or summarised first. Tracks are rendered once with the system voice and speech rate you choose, then saved on your device, so they play offline, in the background, and from the Lock Screen.

### Lets you follow along

Now Playing shows the transcript and highlights roughly where the voice is; tap any sentence to jump there. Pick a speed from 0.75× to 2×, scrub anywhere, skip 15 seconds from the Lock Screen or CarPlay. A phone call pauses playback, and it picks up again when you hang up.

### Builds playlists from a sentence

Make playlists by hand and drag them into order — or describe what you're after ("a tour of the audio pipeline", "short docs under 5 minutes") and Apple Intelligence picks and orders the tracks from your library, names the playlist, and tells you why. You see it before you save it. The model only ever sees your track titles, never their contents.

### Keeps your place in long narrations

Already have narrated audio made elsewhere — a long document rendered to MP3s? Import the folder from Files. Repocast plays the parts as one continuous timeline, shows their transcripts, and picks up a few seconds before where you stopped, so you never lose the thread.

### Catches the thought while you listen

Something you just heard sparks an idea. Tap the mic: playback pauses, Repocast records a voice note pinned to that exact moment, and transcribes it on your device. Later, in the Notes tab, tap the note and you're back at the line that sparked it.

### Comes with you in the car

Repocast supports CarPlay. Your tracks, playlists and narrations show up on the car's screen and drive the same player as your iPhone. Now Playing has a mic button, so you can record a note at the wheel without touching your phone.

## Privacy

Repocast is built so that what you listen to stays yours. The audio is made on your iPhone, from files that travel directly between your iPhone and GitHub.

- Text-to-speech, code summaries, smart playlists and voice-note transcription all run on your device.
- We run no servers. Repository files go straight from GitHub to your iPhone — never through us. Repocast only ever reads from GitHub; it never writes to it.
- Your GitHub token is stored in the iOS Keychain and only ever sent to GitHub. Public repositories need no token at all.
- Voice notes — the recording and its transcript — never leave your device.
- No Repocast account. No advertising IDs. No tracking across apps. No data brokers.
- The one thing we measure is anonymous, aggregate app usage — which tabs you open and which features get used — through Firebase Analytics, so we know what matters. It never includes repository names, file contents, transcripts or notes. [Details in the privacy policy.](https://bclgapps.com.au/repocast-privacy)

## The five tabs

**Tracks** — your library, grouped by repository, plus imported narrations. Make a new track from pasted text, or import a narration folder from Files.

**Repos** — add public repositories by link, or pick from your own once GitHub is connected. Browse folders, tick files, generate.

**Playlists** — hand-built playlists you can reorder, and smart playlists Apple Intelligence builds from a one-line description. Play All or Shuffle.

**Notes** — every voice note, newest first, with its transcript. Tap one to jump back to the moment you recorded it.

**Settings** — connect or disconnect GitHub, choose the voice and speech rate, and see whether Apple Intelligence is ready on this device.

## Requirements

- iPhone or iPad running **iOS 26** or later.
- An **internet connection** to add repositories and fetch files. Tracks you've generated play offline.
- An **Apple Intelligence-eligible device**, with Apple Intelligence turned on, for code summaries and smart playlists. Without it, files are read aloud word for word and you build playlists by hand.
- **Microphone access** for voice notes, and a device and language that support on-device speech recognition for their transcripts.
- A **GitHub account** only if you want to browse your own or private repositories. Public repositories work without one.
- **CarPlay** is optional.

---

## A note on AI summaries

Code summaries and smart playlists are written by a small language model that runs on your iPhone. It's good at the gist, and it can still miss a detail or get one wrong. Treat a summary as a guided tour of a file, and check the source before you act on it. Markdown and text files from a repository are never summarised — they're read word for word — and for text you paste, you choose.
