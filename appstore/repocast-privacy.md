# Repocast Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026_

Repocast is built privacy-first. Repocast turns selected files from a git repository (source code and Markdown) into audio you can play in the car or while exercising, using on-device Apple Intelligence to summarize code, convert docs to speech, generate AI playlists, and voice the latest GitHub changes like new merges and open PRs.

## What stays on your device
Your data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData. It is not uploaded to our servers.

## What we collect
Repocast uses **Firebase Analytics** to collect anonymous, non-identifying usage telemetry — which screens and features are used — to guide what we improve. We never collect your content, and we do not use this data to track you across apps or websites.

## Third parties
- **Google Firebase Analytics** — anonymous usage analytics. See Google's privacy policy.
